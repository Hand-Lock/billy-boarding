# Billy Boarding — spec

## Vision

Small 3D props become **sprites**, the way DOS-era 3D games drew them. Billy
Boarding is a resource pack that swaps the models of cakes, flower pots,
anvils, bells, brewing stands, cactus, crops and fire for flat cross geometry
textured with sprites. Mode 13h then billboards them: each sprite turns to
face the camera.

## Principles

- **Opinionated.** One look, no variants or options.
- **Vanilla JSON only.** Blockstates, block models and textures. No OptiFine
  CEM/CTM, no mod-only features beyond the `render_type` hint (ADR 0002).
- **Item icons as sprites.** Reuse the vanilla item icon when it reads well;
  draw new art only where no icon fits (cake bites, candle cakes, anvils,
  pot layers).
- **Still usable without the shader.** Without Mode 13h the blocks are plain
  crossed sprites, like vanilla flowers. Nothing goes invisible or garbled.

## Mode 13h contract

ADR 0003. The shader side lives in `mode-13h`: `shaders/block.properties`
and `shaders/gbuffers_terrain.vsh`.

- Every billboarded block is listed under **ID 10990** in Mode 13h's
  `block.properties`, gated by its `BILLY_BOARDING` option (default off).
- Its model must be vanilla `block/cross` geometry: two vertical planes from
  0.8 to 15.2, rotated 45° around y with `rescale`, faces with horizontal
  normals only. The shader keeps the face whose normal points to (+x, +z),
  drops the rest, and rotates it to face the camera.
- The shader finds the quad center from UVs, so each face maps the full
  sprite (uv 0–16) and sprites are 16×16. Vertical offsets are fine (pots).
- Wall-mounted bells are flat plates on the wall and are **not** in 10990.
  Only `attachment=floor` and `attachment=ceiling` bells are.
- Changing which blocks have cross models means changing the 10990 list in
  the same session. Renumbering 10990 is a breaking change on both sides.

## Known gaps

- **Colored candle cakes.** The 16 colored candle cake models point at
  `block/candle_cake_<color>_{lit,unlit}`, which neither the pack nor
  vanilla has, so they show the missing texture. `tools/check.sh` reports
  them.
- **Candles, sea pickles, pitcher crop.** They are in Mode 13h's 10990 list
  but have no cross model here, so turning `BILLY_BOARDING` on mangles their
  vanilla geometry.
- **Potted fern.** Its plant layer has no `tintindex`, so the fern should
  render untinted gray. Unverified in game.
- **Fabric render layers.** Fabric ignores `render_type`, so blocks that
  vanilla draws in the solid layer (anvils, cakes at least) may show the
  sprite's transparent pixels as opaque. Unverified in game.

## Roadmap

Each item gets an ADR if it decides something new.

- **R1 — Close the gaps** above: colored candle cake art, cross models for
  candles, sea pickles and the pitcher crop (or drop them from 10990), fern
  tint.
- **R2 — 1.21.1**: new `pack_format`, check renamed blocks and models.
- **R3 — First Modrinth release**, with `tools/release.sh`.

## Non-goals

OptiFine CEM/CTM, mod-specific models or blocks, 3D or voxel models, options
or variants of the look.
